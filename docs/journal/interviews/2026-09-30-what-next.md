# What next for zpkt: an interview

*Written 30 September 2026, straight after the `epoch-0-spine` tag. Answer inline and commit; the answers become the plan.*

Anthony, this is a set of questions about what zpkt does next. Your answers become the plan. Write under each question, as short or long as you like. Skip any that do not matter.

The shape you gave is clear. First, release the HW002 Short. Then pull other projects toward release, stage by stage. The questions follow that order.

## 1. Releasing the HW002 Short

### What "released" means

1. Where does the Short go? YouTube Shorts only, or other places too?
2. Is the Short 30 seconds, 60 seconds, or something else?
3. Does a full-length HW002 release come with it, or later?
4. Is there video? If so, what: a static image, the visual loop, footage?
5. What has to be true for you to press publish?

### Which version

6. Which set is the source: HW002_121, HW002_16, the one-minute demo, or another?
7. Which section should the Short use? The 30-second cut took bars 77–84 and 93–104. Keep it, or choose again?
8. The breakdown hit at bar 81 is about 9 dB above the kicks. Should it be tamed, kept, or replaced?
9. Are any mix changes pending that only you can make?

### Sound and master

10. When will COLDFIRE and LFOTool be activated on the Mac mini? Nothing final renders without them.
11. What loudness do you want? The lab suggests −14 to −11 LUFS, with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling.
12. Who masters it: you, the engineer's hypothesis runs, or both, with you picking?
13. Which three to five reference tracks should the master be judged against?

### How you want to decide

14. How should candidate versions reach you? The /skrng page, your phone, or Live itself?
15. How many versions do you want to compare at once?
16. What may the system change without asking? What must it never touch?

### When

17. Is there a date you want the Short out by?
18. How many hours a week can you give it until then?

## 2. Pulling projects toward release

### Which projects

19. After HW002, which projects come next? Candidates include HW003, HW004, HW005, STRK001, HMSC001, and lift candidates on the experimental shelf.
20. Should any be dropped or demoted now?
21. Do you want one project at a time, or several moving at once?

### What "toward release" means at each stage

22. For each stage, what would progress look like? The stages are sketch, sound design, arrange, mix, master and deliver.
23. Where do you get stuck most often?
24. Where would help save you the most time?
25. Which stages do you want to keep fully for yourself?

### Release as a habit

26. What release cadence feels right: a Short a week, a month, a track a season?
27. Where do releases go besides YouTube? Netlabels, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, DJ sets?
28. Should this site get a page per release?

## 3. Taste and your time

29. How much time a week can you spend answering picks?
30. When during the day do picks fit best?
31. Is a two-button "which would you post?" on /skrng the right shape?
32. Would you rather answer pairs, rank three, or rate one at a time?

## 4. How zpkt should run

### Autonomy

33. How much should the system do overnight without you?
34. What should wake you up, and what should wait for a summary?
35. Should results land as notes on this site, as messages, or both?

### Publishing

36. What may agents publish without asking first? Notes only, renders on /skrng, nothing?
37. Should zpkt stay public?

### The prototypes

38. Is the audio browser part of the release path, for example for picking samples?
39. Should the humming transcriber feed sketches for the next project?

## 5. Anything else

40. What would make you say, in three months, that zpkt worked?
41. What should it stop doing?
42. Is there anything this list missed?
