# HW002 batches 6 and 7: decisions and the new batch format (7 October 2026)

From Anthony's review of batches 6 and 7.1-7.6 on /skrng. Written by Claude Code.

**Status (7 October 2026):** recorded; player changes and batch 8 in progress.

## Decisions on the music

- **Batch 6: "all" is the new baseline.** All four changes together (deep sub, mono low end,
  colour, space) are better than the set as mixed. No single change is better on its own, so
  none is adopted alone. The set is `HW002_121_pp_x_d8x32_b6_all`.
- **Batch 7.1, kick distortion: tracks 3 and 4 are the best of the alternatives.** That is
  Decapitator drive on rumble and COLDFIRE Distortion A drive on the kick group, moved together,
  at 37% and 50%. Track 3 is the first of the increasingly extreme versions; tracks 5 and 6
  (77%, 100%) are worse. The target range for the next test is 37-50%.
- **Colour and space need synths.** Batches 7.5 and 7.6 changed loops of kicks and hats only.
  Colour and space have to be tried on loops that contain synth sounds. In HW002 the synths
  (Break group: "sound 4 synth", "sound 1 synth horn") play only in the break, bars 81-96,
  where there is no kick, so the test loops have to be built, and the processing has to sit
  on a bus the synths pass through.

## The batch format (apply from batch 8)

- **Three alternatives**, at the two ends and the centre of a *target range*. Never outside it.
  The broad sweep (batch 7's six versions from 0 to 2.5 times the change) was less useful.
- **A/B inside one track.** Not the original as one track and each alternative as another.
  Each track switches between the original and one alternative every 1 or 2 bars: on, off,
  on, off. A round-robin track (the original, then each alternative in turn) also works.
  The batch 4.3-6 format, 4-8 bars of one then 4-8 bars of the other, is too slow.
- **Explain the experiment once, at the top.** Then introduce each alternative with a plain
  "Track N". The long measured titles did not help.

## The player

- **Play batch keeps going** into the next older batch at the end of a batch, and on from there.
  It does not stop at the end of the batch.
- **Car transport controls over Bluetooth must work**: play, pause, next and previous, also
  outside a voice review.
