# prototypes

Experiments beside the main path. None is needed to use `hands` or `ears`.

| Folder | The question | State |
|---|---|---|
| [`audio-browser/`](audio-browser/) | Can I listen through a sound library once and decide what each sound is for? A triage app with a collage editor. | Runs. Its collage player mixes in the browser, which I have since ruled out ([contrib/docs/sound.md](../contrib/docs/sound.md)). |
| [`taste/`](taste/) | Which version would I pick? A preference judge over my picks. | Runs; the corpus holds five seed verdicts and no real picks yet. |
| [`engineer/`](engineer/) | What should be tried next, unattended? A loop over hands, ears and taste, and a genetic search. | Tests pass; not wired into anything. |
| [`humming/`](humming/) | Can a hummed idea become notes, tempo and key? A transcriber and benchmarks on HumTrans. | Research; results written up, no CLI. |
| [`soundfunction/`](soundfunction/) | What decides a sound's job in hard techno? Measures, a corpus pipeline, knob-to-measure maps. | Research record. Scripts carry paths from the machine they ran on. |
| [`archive/`](archive/) | The batch, bench and sweep scripts `hands` grew out of (Sep to Oct 2026), with their results. | Does not run. Kept for the method and the numbers. |
