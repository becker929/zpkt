From references to release

Read it west to east. Each stage is a territory; each piece of work is a place in it, coloured by whether it exists. Roads show what feeds what. Every territory drains into the river along the south: the recipe, one arrangement file of structure that the master step runs in Ableton Live.

On a phone, scroll the map sideways.
Through every stage: the recipe file, ears (measure), hands (act in Live), taste (your preferences), and the sound rule (pages play renders and never make sound, apart from named exceptions).
Three ways to hear a change: offline renders from Live, rendered parts started together in the browser, and small batches rendered in Live over ranges you pick.
