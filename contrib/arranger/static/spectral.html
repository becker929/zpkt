Kick fuzz from HW002's own chain, at the same loudness

Each option changes devices already in HW002's kick chain, bounced through it, with the kick channel turned so the kick is as loud as before. The references' kicks are isolated by the beat (the median over every beat of the drop, from the kick onset to the next sixteenth; kickiso.py), and our mix the same way. The chart shows the isolated kick's shape (energy per range, dB of its total): green is the four references' range, white is this option, the hollow mark is the kick as it is. Above about 5 kHz on-beat hats leak into every isolated kick, ours and theirs, so read fizz loosely. Mix: the whole mix with this kick. Kick alone: four bars of kick and rumble, looped.

Every sound here is a render of the mix set in Live (spectral.py), or a reference as released. The page only plays the files and switches between them on the beat. Matched turns each file to the same loudness over the drop, as a listening aid; the references stay far louder and denser than the mix until mastering. A reference plays at its own tempo, its drop lined up with bar 17. Keep writes the fix into the recipe (the arrangement's spectral), for Live to apply.
